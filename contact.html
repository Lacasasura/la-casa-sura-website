FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the minimum stay?

A minimum stay of three nights applies — so you have time to arrive, settle in and truly take the time for yourself, without having to rush.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. In case of late arrivals, self check-in is possible.

Can I join the yoga practices as a drop-in guest?

Yes. Our daily practices are open to in-house guests and drop-in guests.

Do you offer airport transfers?

We work with a trusted local airport transfer service who can pick you up from the airport and bring you directly to La Casa Sura — and take you back to the airport for your departure. Transfer arrangements can be made upon request.

How can I book a cabana?

Choose your dates and preferred cabana through the availability calendar. Availability and the total price are shown before booking.

Can I choose the bed configuration?

Each cabana can be configured as king-size, twin or triple, depending on your needs and availability.

How do I reserve a yoga class?

Reserve through the weekly schedule. We kindly ask you to pay cash on site.

How do I book an upcoming retreat?

Each retreat is independently hosted. Use the Learn More link on the retreat page to visit the host’s website or booking page.

Can I host my own retreat?

Yes. Get in touch and receive our Retreat Host Information Pack.

How many guests can stay?

Up to 11 guests can stay across the four cabanas, with flexible sleeping configurations.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 30 days before arrival.
Cancellations or changes made within 30 days of arrival are non-refundable. In case of a no-show, the full booking amount will be charged.
